See bookings in your phone's calendar

Subscribe once and new bookings show up in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook on their own. Calendar apps check for changes every few hours, so we also email you each new booking straight away.

iPhone (Apple Calendar)

Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar. Paste the link.

Google Calendar

On a computer: calendar.google.com → Other calendars → + → From URL. Paste the link. It then appears on your phone too.

Outlook

Add calendar → Subscribe from web. Paste the link.
