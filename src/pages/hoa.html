Questions boards ask

The short answers

Does it cost the association anything?

No. There's no fee and no contract. Residents pay the pro for the jobs they book, and the association pays nothing.

Is the HOA responsible for the work?

No. Residents book and pay the pro through Neighborhood Perks, and the association isn't a party to those jobs. Sharing us with residents is like sharing any other community resource.

Does anyone on the board get paid?

No. We never pay board members or managers. If your community wants a give-back, any contribution goes to the association itself, in writing, so the board can disclose it.

Can residents still use their own contractors?

Of course. We're an option for residents, never a requirement.

Which services can residents book?

Window re-screening, dog walking and pet sitting today, with house cleaning and more on the way. Tell us what your residents ask for and we'll prioritize it.
