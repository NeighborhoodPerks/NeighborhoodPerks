How much does a house cleaning cost?

It depends on the kind of clean and the size of your home. Each team sets its own prices and shows a "from" price when you choose them, with their full price guide on their profile. Your card is only charged after the clean, for the price your team confirms.

Which kind of clean should I book?

If your home hasn't been cleaned professionally in a while, start with a deep clean or a one-time detailed clean. After that, a regular clean every week or two keeps it that way.

Can I book cleanings on a regular schedule?

Yes. Book each visit you'd like, and they all show up in your account, where you can move or cancel any of them.

How far ahead do I need to book?

Each team sets how much notice they need, and the booking page only shows times you can actually book.

Which areas do you cover?

Carmel Valley, Torrey Highlands and the neighborhoods around them, plus more of San Diego County from Carlsbad and Encinitas to La Jolla, Poway and Santee. Enter your ZIP on the booking page to see who cleans near you.
