Questions

Window re-screening FAQ

How much does window re-screening cost?

Each pro sets their own price, shown as a "from" price when you choose a pro. The final price depends on how many screens you have and their size, and your pro confirms it when the job is done. You're only charged after that.

Do you replace the whole screen frame?

Re-screening puts new mesh into your existing frames. If a frame is bent or broken, mention it in your booking notes so your pro knows before they arrive.

Can my neighbors book at the same time?

Yes. Re-screening is a job almost every house on a street needs, so it's a good one to book together. Mention your neighbors or your street in the booking notes.

Which neighborhoods do you cover?

Carmel Valley, Torrey Highlands, Pacific Highlands Ranch, Del Mar Mesa, Torrey Hills and Rancho Peñasquitos in San Diego. Enter your ZIP on the booking page to see the pros who work on your street.
