Questions

Dog walking FAQ

How much does a dog walk cost?

Each walker sets their own price, shown when you choose a walker. Your card is only charged after the walk, for the price your walker confirms.

How long is each walk?

Each walker sets their own walk length, and you'll see it when you pick a time. If your dog needs something specific, add it in your booking notes.

Can I book walks every week?

Yes. Book as many walks as you need, and they'll all show up in your account, where you can move or cancel any of them.

Which neighborhoods do you cover?

Carmel Valley, Torrey Highlands, Pacific Highlands Ranch, Del Mar Mesa, Torrey Hills and Rancho Peñasquitos in San Diego. Enter your ZIP on the booking page to see who walks dogs on your street.
