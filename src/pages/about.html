Why we do it

Finding someone reliable for every job around the house takes time. You make calls, compare quotes, wait for callbacks and hope they show up. We started Neighborhood Perks so you don't have to.

We bring home services and pet care together in one place, with more coming. We vet the pros, keep their calendars, and group nearby homes together. That makes each trip worth more to the pro, and each job cheaper for you.
