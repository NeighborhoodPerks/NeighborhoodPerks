Questions

Pet sitting FAQ

How much does pet sitting cost?

Each sitter sets their own price per visit, shown when you choose a sitter. Your card is only charged after each visit, for the price your sitter confirms.

How do I book visits for a whole trip?

Book one visit for each day you'll be away. They all show up in your account, where you can move or cancel any of them.

How will my sitter get in?

Add gate codes, key or lockbox details and your pet's routine in the booking notes. Only your sitter and our team see them.

Which neighborhoods do you cover?

Carmel Valley, Torrey Highlands, Pacific Highlands Ranch, Del Mar Mesa, Torrey Hills and Rancho Peñasquitos in San Diego. Enter your ZIP on the booking page to see the sitters near you.
